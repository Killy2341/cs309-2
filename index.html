Emerald Glow

A study in contrast, exploring bold colors and quiet emotion. The striking green skin against the vibrant red backdrop draws the eye to the subject's distant gaze.

The Golden Hour

A portrait focusing on dramatic lighting. The warmth of the golden hair and skin contrasts sharply with the deep, mysterious shadow across the face.

Verdant Soul

An exploration of unconventional color palettes. The vivid pinks and reds of the subject's skin create a surreal, almost botanical atmosphere.

Winter's Breath

A soft, atmospheric piece capturing a moment of quiet reflection. The use of red and blue creates a sense of cold air and warm comfort simultaneously.

Perception

A surreal, monochromatic piece exploring the concept of sight. The layered imagery asks the viewer to look deeper into the subject's expression.

Midnight Melancholy

A digital painting focusing on mood and lighting. The deep blues and sharp highlights create a hauntingly beautiful portrait.
